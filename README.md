# WordSv 🇸🇪

A WordUp-style app for learning Swedish vocabulary at every level (A1–C1) with spaced repetition. It runs in the browser and installs on iPhone as a home-screen app (PWA), with no build step and no backend.

## How it works

- **Learn**: on first launch you pick the CEFR levels you want (A1–C1). New words come from those levels, easiest first. Swipe right (or tap *Add*) to add a word to your vocabulary. Swipe left to skip it.
- **Review**: words come back on this schedule (days): `1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 14, 21, 31, 60, 90, 145, 180`.
  - Swipe right: the word moves to the next step.
  - Swipe left: the word goes 2 steps back and comes up again tomorrow.
  - After the 180-day step, the word is marked as mastered.
- **My words**: search your words and see each word's progress and next review date. Switch to **All words** to search the whole bank (Swedish or English) and add words directly.
- **Moments** (sign-in required): pick a public username and avatar, add friends by username, and share the words you learn as styled, Instagram-style text stories that last 24 hours (8 colour themes, 5 fonts, emoji, alignment), or take a **photo with the camera** (or pick one from the library) and put your word on top of it. Tap a friend's ring to watch their stories, react with emoji, and add their word to your own list with one tap. Includes a weekly friends leaderboard.
- **Dagens ord**: a word of the day from your levels, in Learn and Moments.
- **Me**: stats plus 20 badges (streaks, word counts, mastered words, friends, moments…) with confetti when you unlock one. Settings are under Me → ⚙︎.
- **Stats**: daily streak, reviews per day, what's coming up in the next 7 days, how strong your words are, and progress per level.
- **Undo**: every swipe can be taken back for a few seconds (handy after a mis-swipe).
- **I know it**: in Learn, marks a word you already know; it starts at step 6 and comes back in 2 weeks.
- **Review direction**: Swedish → English, English → Swedish, or mixed (Settings).
- **Keyboard** (web on a computer): ← / → to swipe, space to reveal, K = I know it, U = undo.
- **Settings**: turn levels on/off for Learn, daily review limit, auto-read aloud, add your own words, export/import a backup.

Progress is stored in your browser on that device (`localStorage`). Optionally, **sign in with Apple or Google** (Settings → Account & sync, or on the welcome screen) to back up your progress and sync it between devices. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).

## Sign-in & sync (Firebase)

- Apple and Google sign-in use Firebase Authentication (Apple needs a Services ID and key from an Apple Developer account, set up under Firebase → Authentication → Sign-in method → Apple); progress is stored in Cloud Firestore, one document per user at `users/{uid}`.
- `src/firebase-config.js` holds the public web config. `firestore.rules` holds the security rules (paste them into Firebase console → Firestore Database → Rules → Publish).
- Sync merges instead of overwriting: every word has a change time, removals leave a tombstone, and the newest change wins per word (`src/merge.js`, tested in `tests/merge.test.js`). Syncs run a few seconds after changes, when the app opens or comes back to the foreground, and on “Sync now”.
- Social data lives in `usernames/{name}`, `profiles/{uid}`, `friendships/{uidA_uidB}` and `moments/{id}`; `firestore.rules` enforces unique usernames, that only friends at posting time can read a moment, and that friends can only add their own reaction.
- Photos are cropped to 9:16, resized and JPEG-compressed in the browser (`src/photo.js`, max ~280 KB) and stored inline in the moment document. This keeps the project on the free Spark plan; Cloud Storage for Firebase needs the paid Blaze plan.
- The Firebase SDK is loaded from Google's CDN only when someone signs in, so the app stays light for everyone else.

## Install on iPhone

1. Open the GitHub Pages URL in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**.
3. Always open WordSv from the home-screen icon. It keeps its own storage, separate from Safari tabs.

## Word lists

- `data/words.json`: 104 hand-picked B2/C1 words with example sentences written for this app.
- `data/kelly.json`: all A1–C1 words from the **Swedish Kelly list** (6,930 words, ordered by level and frequency), with English translations, inflections and example phrases from **Folkets lexikon**. It is generated by `tools/build_kelly.py`. Translations for the ~270 words Folkets lacks are in `tools/kelly_extra_translations.json`. Every word has example sentences: for about 2,900 words they were written for this app (`tools/examples/*.tsv`, which also correct a few dictionary translations), and the rest come from Folkets lexikon.

Sources and licences:
- Swedish Kelly list: Volodina & Johansson Kokkinakis (2012), Språkbanken Text, University of Gothenburg. CC BY 4.0. https://spraakbanken.gu.se/en/resources/kelly
- Folkets lexikon (The People's Dictionary), KTH. CC BY-SA 2.5. https://folkets-lexikon.csc.kth.se/folkets/
- Bricolage Grotesque typeface by Mathieu Triay, SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`fonts/OFL.txt`). Subset in `fonts/bricolage.woff2`.

Because it includes Folkets lexikon content, `data/kelly.json` is shared under **CC BY-SA 2.5**.

## Adding words

Edit `data/words.json`. Each entry looks like this:

```json
{
  "id": "unique-id",
  "sv": "ord",
  "en": "word",
  "pos": "noun",
  "forms": "ett ord, -et, -",
  "level": "B2",
  "examples": [{ "sv": "Ett svenskt exempel.", "en": "An English translation." }]
}
```

`id` must be unique and must never change (your progress is linked to it). You can also add words from inside the app, in Settings.

## Tests

```bash
npm test   # Node 18+; tests the review schedule and stats logic
```

The GitHub Pages workflow runs the tests before every deploy.

## Run locally

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
# open http://localhost:8000
```

## Deploy (GitHub Pages)

Repo **Settings → Pages → Build and deployment → Source: GitHub Actions**. After that, every push to `main` publishes the app automatically (see `.github/workflows/deploy.yml`). The workflow also generates the PNG icons with `tools/make_icons.py`.
